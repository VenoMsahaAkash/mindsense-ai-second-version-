# Emergency Crisis Protocols & Safety Intervention

## Crisis Assessment & Response
When an individual presents with acute distress, suicidal ideation, or self-harm intent:
1. **Empathetic Grounding**: Validate feelings without judgment or alarm. Speak with warmth and stability.
2. **Safety First**: Directly ask about safety while providing active emergency crisis helplines.
3. **Pacing**: Help the individual focus on surviving just the next hour or next step, rather than solving all life problems at once.
