# Dialectical Behavior Therapy (DBT) Skills Guide

## Overview of DBT
Dialectical Behavior Therapy (DBT) combines cognitive-behavioral approaches with mindfulness principles. It emphasizes finding a balance between **acceptance** of oneself and **change** in behavior.

## Core Modules & Techniques

### 1. TIPP Skills (Distress Tolerance)
When emotional arousal is extremely high (8-10/10), TIPP skills quickly reset the autonomic nervous system:
- **Temperature**: Splash cold water on your face or hold an ice cube to trigger the mammalian dive reflex and drop heart rate.
- **Intense Exercise**: Engage in short bursts of intense physical activity (jumping jacks, fast walking) to expend fight-or-flight energy.
- **Paced Breathing**: Inhale for 4 seconds, exhale for 6-8 seconds. Longer exhales activate the parasympathetic nervous system.
- **Paired Muscle Relaxation**: Tense muscle groups on an inhale, and release completely on an exhale.

### 2. STOP Skill (Crisis Survival)
- **S - Stop**: Pause. Do not react immediately.
- **T - Take a step back**: Disengage physically or mentally from the trigger.
- **O - Observe**: Notice what is happening inside and outside without judgment.
- **P - Proceed mindfully**: Ask yourself what action will make the situation better, not worse.

### 3. Radical Acceptance
- Acknowledging reality as it is in this present moment without fighting or resisting it.
- Accepting a situation does not mean approving of it; it means ceasing the suffering caused by fighting unchangeable facts.
