# General Evidence-Based Coping Strategies

## Grounding & Anxiety Relief
1. **5-4-3-2-1 Sensory Grounding**:
   - 5 things you can **see** around you.
   - 4 things you can physically **feel** (e.g. feet on floor, clothes on skin).
   - 3 things you can **hear**.
   - 2 things you can **smell**.
   - 1 deep, conscious **breath**.

2. **Box Breathing (4x4 Protocol)**:
   - Inhale through nose for 4 seconds.
   - Hold breath for 4 seconds.
   - Exhale through mouth for 4 seconds.
   - Hold empty for 4 seconds.
   - Repeat 4 cycles to lower physiological arousal.

3. **Physiological Sigh**:
   - Two quick inhales through the nose followed by one long, slow exhale through the mouth.
   - Immediately reduces carbon dioxide buildup and calms heart rate.

## Stress & Burnout Management
- **Brain Dump**: Write down all thoughts, obligations, and worries onto paper to reduce cognitive load.
- **Eisenhower Matrix**: Prioritize tasks into Urgent vs Important to prevent feeling overwhelmed.
- **Sleep Hygiene**: Maintain consistent wake/sleep times and avoid blue light screens 1 hour before sleep.
